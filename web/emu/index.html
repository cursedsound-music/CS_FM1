  1. Six tracks: 1 DRUM (the kit, without its kick), 2 KICK, 3 ACID (the 303), 4 BASS, 5 PAD, 6 VOICE. Press Switch on, then - to play and stop. It starts empty: press 9 for an acid line or 0 to write drums.

Simple and deep (0.16.0): each button now goes round only its everyday pages: HOME PERFORM, LIVE · SEQ TRIG, STEP, EUCLID · EDIT the sound and FILTER · ENV AMP, FILTER 1, LFO 1 · FX DJ, SEND, DELAY, REVERB · GEN each generator's first page · GLO MIX, LIMIT.   4. ← → pick the track. Drag the knobs up or right to turn them up, down or left to turn them down (the mouse wheel works too).
  5. On SEQ the white keys (Z X C…) are the 16 trigs; on a drum kit the black keys (S D F…) pick the sound. [ ] flip through banks of 16. On a synth track (ACID, bass, pad) hold a step key and turn the PRESETS knob (drag or scroll it) to set its note up or down the scale (a rest becomes the root first); while holding it, black key 1 (S) toggles ACCENT and black key 2 (D) SLIDE. To empty the track, hold 0 (SEQ) and press = (REC) twice within 3 seconds; REC on the ARP page undoes it.
  6. Acid in one key: press 9 (GEN) and a 303 line is already playing. From the drum track it jumps to the acid track (track 2); if no track is ACID, a synth track becomes one. ] throws a new line, [ goes back to the last one, = (REC) undoes.   7. Dub chords in one key: pick a synth track (HOME, white key 4 is the pad), press 9 (GEN): an empty pad or chord track becomes DUB STAB (the bass and VOICE tracks get an ACID line instead, so GEN never changes their sound) and gets a groove of four-note stabs, mostly on the offbeats, in the song's key. Knobs: CHORD (MIN7, MIN9, MIN11, 7SUS4, SUS2, MIN, MAJ7), DENSITY, MOVE (OFF, 2 or 4 chords: the shape moved in parallel, the dub way), LENGTH (short stab to held). 9 again: BARS (1, 2, 4), VOICING (CLOSE, INV 1, INV 2, OPEN), ECHO (the track's delay and reverb sends), OCTAVE; then ROOT, EVERY and AMOUNT: every 1–16 bars part of the groove changes by itself (on that page ] mutates now, [ goes back to the original). ] throws a new groove, [ goes back. DUB 3's DELAY sets the shared echo to fit the stabs (1/8D, 1/4D, 1/8, 1/4, 1/16D; KEEP leaves it). Further pages are ACID's, for a 303 line on the same track instead. To play chords live: 5 (EDIT) → CHORD page: one key plays a whole chord (MIN7, 7TH, SUS…, VOICING with LEAD for smooth voice leading, STRUM).
  8. HOME is the perform page: white keys 1–6 pick a track, black keys 1–6 mute (they land on the next bar; PERFORM 2's MUTE Q sets NOW, BEAT or BAR), hold 7 (HOME) with a black key to solo. White keys 9–12 (. / Q W) play sections A–D on the next bar. Black key 7 (;) is FILL: a snare roll in the second half of the bar, back to normal after; hold it for a fill every bar. Black key 8 (') is BREAKDOWN: press once and the kick and lows go out on the next bar; again for a 4-bar BUILD (HP and noise risers, a roll at the end); it DROPS on the bar after. Press during the build to drop sooner; FX + [ cancels. PERFORM's knobs (0.10.1): 1 BPM in whole steps, 2 FINE, the hundredths .00 to .99 (130 and .16 is 130.16, shown in the top right), 3 SWING, 4 DJ FILTER. Press HOME again for LIVE (below), again for PERFORM 2: DUCK (the kick pumps the other tracks, the sidechain sound) and its release, MUTE Q, and the track's OCTAVE; again for ISOLATOR: LOW, MID and HIGH, a DJ mixer's isolator (24 dB an octave at 200 Hz and 2.9 kHz), each from KILL through −24 to +6 dB; the black keys 9–11 kill a band while held; again for MACROS: M1–M4, the four macros each sound's MOD pages route (the pad's M2 closes its filter, for example). Black keys 9–11 (E T I) kill the lows, mids or highs while held, like a DJ mixer. Hold HOME and tap white key 16 (O) in time for TAP TEMPO (in tenths of a BPM).
  9. MORPH (two scenes and a glide): HOME › MORPH. White key 13 (R) stores A, white key 14 (Y) stores B: the tracks' levels and delay/reverb sends, the macros, the delay feedback, reverb size, DJ filter and rumble level. Once both are stored, press 13 or 14 to glide there over BARS (1–16, from the next bar; CUT jumps on the bar). Knob 1 (A-B) is the crossfader by hand. HOME + 13 / 14 stores again. Only what A and B hold differently moves.
  10. LOOP (polymeters, density): HOME › LOOP acts on the track picked, without changing its pattern: LEN plays only its first N steps (try 3, 5 or 7 against the others' 16), RATE /4 … x4, DENS thins it out (−90 … −10 %) or ratchets it (x2 … x4), SHIFT moves it by steps. White key 15 (U) brings it back as written, in phase, on the next beat; HOME + 15 brings every track back. A red dot by a track's name means it plays modified.
  11. RUMBLE: 1 (FX) to the last page. The kick (the kits, low-passed) goes into a dark reverb of its own: LEVEL (0 is off), DECAY, TONE (60–250 Hz), DUCK (the rumble ducks at each kick and swells into the offbeat). It goes through the DJ filter, the isolator and the low kill like everything else; KICK KILL lets the tail ring out.
  12. DRUMS, the live drum page (0.13.0, DRONE's place): tap 4 (LFO). Hold a white key 1–8 to roll that drum (KICK SNARE CLAP HATCL HATOP TOM RIM BELL) on the beat's grid; ROLL page: RATE (1/8 1/16 1/32 1/16T 1/32T), RAMP (FLAT, UP, DOWN), VEL. Hold black 1–4 to repeat the drums' last 8 / 4 / 2 / 1 steps (a beat repeat on the kit and the KICK, while playing); let go and they're back where they would have been. CHANCE and CHANCE 2: each drum's chance to play its steps (100 % always … 0 never), kept in the project. Rolls go out over MIDI too (SENDS ALL).
  13. Your own kits (0.13.0): SAVE (8) three times: KITS, K01–K16. Knob 1 the slot; ] (OCT+) saves the kit as it is now (its eight sounds, their levels, envelopes, sends, chance; stopped only; a used slot asks first), [ (OCT−) loads it (playing too), REC twice erases.

KNOBS: FM-1 (as always), +MIDI (every knob also sends its value as a CC: map it in Ableton, absolute), MIDI (the knobs only send, the FM-1's sound stays; map in Ableton as "Relative (2's Comp.)").   15. Syntakt routing: 6 (GLO) through the pages to MIDI: knob 1 the track's MIDI channel, knob 2 what goes out (ALL: the notes too), knob 3 the clock (TRS follows the Syntakt, USB follows Ableton: in Live turn on Sync for the FM-1's output port), knob 4 LOCAL (OFF: the keys only send their notes over USB, on the track's channel, and sound nothing here: the FM-1 as a MIDI keyboard for Ableton). The next page, LINK: set PC CH to the Syntakt's program-change channel and a Syntakt pattern change switches cursed-os's section on the bar (patterns 1, 5, 9, 13 → A; 2, 6, 10, 14 → B; and so on). PC OUT sends a program change when you switch a section here (over USB or Bluetooth; the FM-1's jack is MIDI in only). VJ CH sends the live events as notes for visuals: 60 BREAK, 61 BUILD, 62 DROP, 63 FILL, 64–67 section A–D, and CC 20 rising through the build. The next page, LINK 2, links the four macros to MIDI CCs on MAC CH. MAP picks the Syntakt's CCs: TRACK (set MAC CH to a Syntakt track's channel) is that track's filter frequency 74, resonance 75, delay send 84 and reverb send 85; FX (MAC CH = the Syntakt's FX channel) is the analog FX filter 70, its resonance 71, delay feedback 24 and reverb decay 30. LINK 3 sets each macro's CC by hand (MAP then shows OWN). MO CC puts MORPH's A-B on a CC too: an Octatrack's crossfader, or any fader on that channel, moves the morph, and a glide sends its position out. With DIR on IN or BOTH, the Syntakt's knobs (through the MIDI in jack) turn the macros; with OUT or BOTH, the macros and MORPH send their moves out over USB or Bluetooth. A value that came in is never sent back.

With a scale set, the keys light up as the scale wherever they play a synth (live, STEP INPUT, the KEY page): only the notes in the scale lit, the root brighter, the rest dark (CHR: every key as before).

Sound design on every synth: 5 (EDIT) goes through the engine's two pages, FILTER (every engine's, the kit too: TYPE LP HP BP COMB MS20, CUT, RES, ENV; the same filter as ENV › FILTER 1), VOICE (poly, mono, legato, unison, glide, detune) and four MOD pages, the modulation matrix (source, destination, amount; LFO2 is a source too). 3 (ENV): AMP; FILTER 1 (TYPE LP HP BP COMB MS20, CUT, RES, ENV); FILTER 2, a second filter after it (TYPE OFF LP HP BP NOTCH, CUT, RES, ENV); ENV MOD; LFO 1 (SYNC HZ or BPM, RATE in Hz or 32BAR .. 1/32 (32, 16 and 8 bars for slow, hypnotic sweeps), WAVE, MODE FREE / TRIG / ONE), LFO 1 TO (FILTER, PITCH, SHAPE, AMP), LFO 1+ (PHASE, FADE); LFO 2 (SYNC, RATE, WAVE, MODE) and LFO 2+ (PHASE, to FILTER 2). FREE runs on (in BPM it locks to the bar), TRIG restarts from PHASE on each new note, ONE plays one cycle per note and holds (an extra envelope). Eleven LFO shapes: SIN TRI SAW SQR S&H RAMP PULSE SMOOTH STEPS EXP BOUNCE, drawn on the page; ↑ ↓ step through the pages both ways.
  19. Delay and reverb: tap 1 (FX): SEND is each track's DIST, CHORUS, DLY and REV amount; press 1 again for DIST, DELAY (TIME synced to the tempo, FEEDBACK, TONE, PING-PONG), DELAY 2 (MIX, DRIVE, TYPE, MOD: the tape echo's wow and flutter; FEEDBACK past 100 runs away like a dub spin), DELAY 3 (WIDE: the right echo later for a stereo spread; PITCH and SPRAY: the grain delay, each repeat shifted up or down and scattered), REVERB (SIZE, DAMP, PRE-DELAY, TYPE: PLATE, the default, a lush stereo plate whose SIZE runs from under a second to ~15 s; ROOM, SPRING, SHIMMER, RESO, CLOUD) and REVERB 2 (MOD and RATE: movement in the tail, FILT: the input darker or brighter, WIDE: the stereo width). The delay and reverb are shared by every track.
  20. Drums: on the drum track, 5 (EDIT) edits one drum at a time: strike its key (or turn knob 1, DRUM) to pick it. DRUM (PITCH, BEND, LEVEL), TONE (NOISE, TONE, WAVE, FILTER), TONE 2 (BEND TYPE, DRIVE, LAYER, FEEDBACK), ENV (ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN, RELEASE: with SUSTAIN on, the drum rings while its step or key is held, then releases; off, it is a one-shot), ENV 2 (HOLD, SNAP), SEND (its own DELAY, REVERB, CHORUS: KIT follows the kit's FX › SEND, 0 keeps that drum dry, so the kick can stay out of the reverb). FX pad (for techno): hold 1 (FX) and hold white keys: each effect runs while its key is held (red = on), let go and it stops. 1–3 REPEAT 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 · 4–5 GATE 1/16, 1/8 (chops the whole mix on the grid) · 6 TAPE STOP · 7 GLITCH · 8–9 LPF / HPF sweeps · 10 DRIVE · 11 CRUSH · 12 DELAY THROW (the mix into the delay, feedback up; let go and the echoes ring on) · 13 REVERB WASH · 14 FREEZE (the delay holds what it has) · 15 HP RISER and 16 NOISE RISER (both climb over 4 bars; let go = the drop). The four knobs while FX is held: FILTER, CRUSH, THROW, DEPTH. FX + ] switches to latch (a press turns an effect on, the next off), FX + [ turns everything off. Held, FX lights every key whose effect is on (latched or held), so you can turn them off without missing.   23. Beatmatch: hold 7 (HOME) and hold [ or ] to nudge the tempo 3 % slower or faster while held, like a Syntakt's TEMPO and arrows (the BPM turns red). While HOME is held the screen shows PERFORM, so knob 1 is always BPM and knob 2 FINE (knob 1 moves in tenths while HOME is held); let go and you're back on the page you were on (a quick tap on HOME turns to its next page). Projects keep the hundredths. Following an external clock (GLO › MIDI › CLOCK), the header shows the clock's tempo, steadied, and the nudge, tap, BPM and FINE knobs say EXT CLOCK LEADS: nudge the machine that sends the clock.
  24. Master: 6 (GLO), then ↓ through the pages. COMP: ON, THRESH (0 to −40 dB), RATIO (1.5:1 to 20:1), MAKEUP. COMP 2: INPUT (±12 dB), ATTACK, RELEASE, KNEE. COMP 3: SC HPF (the detector ignores the lows, so the kick doesn't pump the rest) and MIX (under 100 = parallel compression). LIMIT: DRIVE (up to +12 dB into the limiter), CEILING (the peak that comes out, −6 to −18 dBFS), RELEASE, AUTO LV (JIANT's leveler, which rides the loudness; turn it OFF so MAKEUP and DRIVE stay as you set them). Each page has a gain-reduction meter. GLO's next four pages measure the output as it leaves, after the COMP and LIMIT (what the headphones and USB get). SCOPE: the wave, TIME 5, 11, 44 or 87 ms, GAIN AUTO or x1–x8, TRIG on a rising zero crossing, MODE MONO, L/R (left white, right red) or M/S (mid white, side red). SPECTRUM: 32 bands from 30 Hz to 20 kHz, VIEW bars with peak marks or a waterfall over ~5 s, RANGE 60/48/36 dB, SPEED of the fall, SOURCE MID or SIDE (what is only in the stereo). STEREO: a vectorscope (a mono sound is a line straight up, a wide one a cloud, out of phase lies flat), the correlation (+1 mono, 0 wide, below 0 red: parts cancel in mono), WIDTH and the balance; ZOOM. LOUDNESS (EBU R128 / BS.1770): MOMENTARY (400 ms), SHORT (3 s) and INTEGRATED (gated, since RESET) in LUFS, the sample PEAK in dBFS, a bar of the short-term against a TARGET (−14 for streaming, −9 to −6 for a loud club master); turn RESET to start the integrated reading again. After that MIDI and SYSTEM (TRACKS 4, 5 or 6, LOAD, BT, IDLE: OFF or 1 to 60 minutes, 1 MIN the default; it also shows the battery's estimate, volts and raw reading).
  25. Battery and idle screen: the header shows the battery in percent between the engine and the key (USB when a computer powers it; red under 15 %, and BATTERY LOW once under 10 %). The percentage is an estimate until calibrated. Untouched for the IDLE time, the screen becomes an oscilloscope of the master (its gain automatic). The sound keeps playing; the first touch only wakes the screen and does nothing else.

BLEEPS, modular style (0.16.1): GEN > BLEEP now writes a short MOTIF (BLEEP 2, knob 1: 3, 5, 6 or 7 steps; 5 by default) that loops against the 4/4 and shifts across the bar, the classic hypnotic polyrhythm; OFF gives the old 1–4 bar lines. BLEEP 3: EVERY (a small mutation every N bars), SLIDE, RATCHET, ECHO (delay send, the reverb almost as much). BLEEP 4: DRIFT (over about 48 bars the filter opens a little, the pings ring a little longer and the echo gets denser, then it slowly comes back; restarts at each PLAY), SPACE (sets the shared delay to PING-PONG at 1/8D, 1/4D, 1/8 or 1/16D with feedback 65; KEEP leaves it), SOUND. The sounds are struck, not played: instant onset with a tiny trigger tick, a short natural fall, through a low-pass gate that follows the note and darkens as the ping fades, and a pitch that wanders a few cents. New WAVEs: WOOD (a struck bar), GLASS (bell partials that beat), DROP (a water drop, the pitch rising as it falls). New sounds: SONAR PING, WATER DROP, CAVE DRIP, GLASS PING, WOOD BLOCK, R2 BLIPS, METAL TICK, next to BLEEP SINE, RANDOM PINGS and BLOOP DROP.
  39. BLEEPS (0.15.1): GEN > BLEEP on any synth track writes a line of bleeps in the key from a SEED: the same seed is always the same line (turn it, or OCT+ for a new one, OCT- back). BLEEP: SEED, DENSITY, RANGE (how many scale notes), OCTAVE. BLEEP 2: LENGTH (1/2/4 bars), VARY (how much the later bars differ), ACCENT, SLIDE. BLEEP 3: RATCHET, ECHO (the delay send), EVERY (mutates every N bars; OCT+ here mutates now, OCT- back), SOUND (loads one of the BLEEP sounds). The sound: DUB's TYPE BLEEP, eight presets (BLEEP SINE, BLOOP DROP, FM BLIP, GLASS PING, MODULAR TRI, FOLD PLUCK, RANDOM PINGS, SQUARE BLEEP) with deep knobs: WAVE (SINE TRI SQUARE FM FOLD), COLOR, FM, DECAY, RATIO, BLOOP (the pitch drops into the note from above, or rises from below), RANDOM (each note its own FM, decay, bloop and ratio, the same every pass), plus the track's FILTER, LFO and MOD pages.
  40. DUB (0.15.0), dub chords and atmospheres: a new engine (ALGORITHM: DUB, after ACID). EDIT: TYPE (STAB or ATMOS), CHORD (MIN7 MIN9 MIN MIN11 SUS4 5TH: one key plays the whole chord), COLOR (darker … brighter), DENSITY (emptier … denser), DECAY (STAB: the filter's fall; ATMOS: MOTION, how fast it drifts), RES, THROW (STAB: the chance a stab is thrown into the delay), NOISE (air). STAB presets: DUB STAB, THROW STAB, DEEP CHORD, BASIC CHORD, MINOR 11. ATMOS presets: ATMOS DARK, WIDE, VOID, GLOW: hold a key (or write one long step) and it evolves by itself; whatever key you press, the chord sits on the track's ROOT (SCL > ROOT), so it is always in key. Its presets bring their delay / reverb sends (the other engines load dry); set REVERB TYPE to CLOUD or TIGHT for the big washes. Bluetooth now shows the FM-1 as "cursed os" (remove the old "JIANT FM-1" pairing on your phone / Mac first).
  41. 0.14.1: with a KICK track (the default), the kit's first drum is PERC (a ride at first; load any sound on EDIT > SOUND): its own mute (HOME + white 9), not in KICK KILL, no DUCK; GEN gives it a percussion line. EDIT > SOUND on a kit: DRUM, SOURCE, PAN, CHANCE (was WAVE and FILTER again, still on TONE). Each drum's SEND is OFF at 0 and adds to the kit's FX > SEND (no KIT); loading a sound no longer turns on chorus, delay or reverb sends: sounds load dry. The SEQ views show the 16ths and the beats. An external clock (Ableton) is measured over a whole beat and smoothed: 135 reads 135.0 and the tempo holds steady.
  42. COND, SLIDE and SOUND locks (0.14.0, Elektron-style): TRIG > COND. Hold one or more steps (white keys) and turn: knob 1 COND, when the step plays: 1ST (first pass only), !1ST, FILL / !FILL (with the FILL), or N:M, pass N of every M (1:2, 2:2, 1:3 … 8:8; e.g. 4:4 for a hit at the end of every 4th bar-loop). It takes the place of the step's chance. Knob 2 SLIDE: the step's locks glide in across the step instead of jumping (lock CUTOFF on a few steps with SLIDE for a sweep); on a synth the note slides too (as B2). Knob 3 SOUND (a kit): pick the drum with the black keys, then any of the 85 drum sounds plays on those steps instead (a clap on step 13, another hat on the off-beats); its own sound is back on its next hit. Conditioned steps are drawn hollow, a sound lock is a red dot. REC + step clears locks and sound locks. On a kit a COND is the whole step's (all its drums).
  43. RING and TIGHT (0.13.1): FX > DIST TYPE gains RING, a ring modulator: the track times a sine, TONE (now FREQ) its pitch, ~20 Hz (a wobble) to ~2.5 kHz (bells, metal), DIST the mix of dry and ring. REVERB TYPE gains TIGHT, a small, dense room for drums after the LXR's reverb: SIZE its length, DAMP darker, PRE the pre-delay, MOD / RATE its shimmer, and WIDE becomes EARLY, the early reflections' level (a snappy room around the hits).
  44. FMDRUM, transients, TUBE and RAT (0.11.0): a drum synth's voices, after the LXR drum machine. FMDRUM (turn ALGORITHM past KICK) is one FM voice for hits: EDIT 1 TYPE (DRUM, SNARE, CYMBAL, HAT), WAVE, SWEEP (how far the pitch falls), S.TIME (how fast; the FM closes with it), EDIT 2 FM (the depth), RATIO (the modulator: 0.5 … 8 and the metallic 1.41, 2.76, 3.73, 5.33), NOISE, M.WAVE. The keys play it in tune (toms, a kick line), the ENV page is its length. Presets: FM KICK, FM TOM, FM SNARE, CLAP-ISH, RIDE, CRASH, CLOSED HAT, OPEN HAT, COWBELL, ZAP PERC. Its third EDIT page, TRANSIENT, adds an attack to each hit: SNAP (a pitch spike) or one of the LXR's twelve attack samples: CLICK, CLICK 2, ATTACK, ACOUST, RIM, DRIP, PEDAL, CLAP, TOY, DIGI 1–3, with T.AMT its level. The KICK track has the same page (KICK, KICK 2, TRANSIENT). FX > DIST TYPE gains TUBE (a valve's warm, lopsided curve) and RAT (the pedal: lots of gain, then two diodes).
  45. BACK TO NORMAL lets the effects ring out (0.12.5): OCT− on LIVE (or the phone's button) stops sending into the delay and reverb (the sends fade in ~0.1 s) but leaves what is already in them to tail out; a raised FEEDBACK glides back to normal over two bars instead of at once. Turn FEEDBACK or a send meanwhile and your move wins. The DJ FILTER and the mutes still snap back (the drop).
  46. A step's length (0.12.4): on SEQ, hold a step's white key (or several) and press black 3 for shorter, black 4 for longer: 1/16, 2/16, 3/16, 4/16, 6/16, 8/16, 10/16, 12/16, 15/16 of a step, then 2, 3 … 16 steps (the steps after it become TIEs, up to the next note). The held box shows LEN; a step shorter than a whole one is drawn narrower in the step row. Without a length set, the track's GATE (SEQ knob 4) decides. In STEP input, a key held + REC still ties.
  47. Sends (every track): DELAY and REVERB per track are on FX > SEND (tap FX, then its second tab: DIST, CHORUS, DLY, REV). The ACID also has them in its own EDIT pages (SEND, after DEVIL: DLY, REV, DIST, CHORUS, 0.12.3). HOME > LIVE's REVERB and DELAY add to every track's at once.
  48. FM kit sounds, more (0.13.0): on a kit sound with FILTER FM, TONE 2's knobs are its S.TIME (SHORT / LONG sweep), RATIO (AUTO, 0.5 … 8, the metallic ones) and WAVE (AUTO, SINE TRI SAW SQUARE). FMDRUM's and KICK's TRANSIENT page gains T.PITCH (−24 … +24 semitones: the attack sample slower and lower, or faster and brighter). The kick and the tom are brought back to full rate more cleanly (less hash at the top).
  49. Any drum sound into any drum (0.12.1): the DRUM track's EDIT has a SOUND page (tap 5 again after DRUM): knob 1 picks the kit's drum (KICK … BELL, or strike its key), knob 2 SOURCE loads any drum sound the firmware has into it: every factory kit's eight (all the snares of the nine kits together, then the hats …: “909 SNR”, “808 HHC”, “TEC CLP”), KICK's own DEEP, PUNCH, RUMBLE, and FMDRUM's ten presets on the FM voice (FM KICK … FM ZAP). Knobs 3–4 are WAVE and FILTER right there. It takes the sound only: the drum's LEVEL, envelope, sends, steps and key stay. Then shape it on the other pages; once edited, SOURCE shows OWN. A 909 kick on the clap's key, an FM hat in place of the open hat, a TECHNO snare in the DRUM-X kit.
  50. FMDRUM sounds inside the kit (0.12.0): any of the kit's eight sounds can play on FMDRUM's voice and keep its place, its steps and its key. On the DRUM track's EDIT, pick the sound with knob 1 on the DRUM page (SNARE, say), then on the TONE page turn knob 4 FILTER to FM. Its knobs then read as FMDRUM's: WAVE becomes TYPE (DRUM, SNARE, CYMBL, HAT), PITCH, BEND is SWEEP, TONE is FM (the depth), NOISE, DECAY and the ENV page as before; SNAP (ENV 2) and BENDTYP (TONE 2) become TRANS, the attack sample (OFF, SNAP, CLICK, CLK 2, RIM, CLAP, PEDAL, ACOUS); DRIVE still drives it. The closed hat still cuts the open one. FILTER back to LP / BP / HP and it is the kit's own sound again. Saved with the kit in the project.
  51. The kick and the drums in and out (0.10.0): the kick has a track of its own, T2 KICK, so it comes in and goes out apart from the kit (T1 DRUM, whose kick lane is at 0). Its EDIT pages: KIND (the kick of each factory kit, 909, 808, TECHNO … and DEEP, PUNCH, RUMBLE), TUNE, TONE, DECAY, CLICK, SWEEP, FOLD, DRIVE; the keys tune it (C as struck), the presets TECHNO, 909, 808 LONG, DEEP, PUNCH, RUMBLE, INDUSTRIAL, DUB. 9 (GEN) on the KICK track writes four on the floor if it is empty, and knob 1 PATTERN throws another (4/4, 4/4+, ROLL, BROKEN, HALF, GALLOP, NO 4, OFF 3). Bringing the drums in one by one: on HOME hold 7 (HOME) and tap white keys 1–8: KICK (the KICK track), SNARE, CLAP, HAT, OPEN HAT, TOM, RIM, BELL, each in or out, on the beat or the bar as MUTE Q says. While HOME is held the line under the tracks shows the eight (struck through: out); let go, it lists what is out. The FX pad's KICK KILL kills the KICK track too.
  52. The phone remote (0.9.5 – 0.10.2): put cursed_remote.py and cursed_remote_proto.py (in the firmware's tools folder) in Pythonista on the iPhone and run cursed_remote.py. LINK: turn Bluetooth on on the FM-1, tap BLUETOOTH MIDI, tap “cursed os”, swipe the window down, then pick it in the list (a USB cable works too). PLAY: PLAY, an exact BPM typed (128.5; tap beside the keys to put the keyboard away, then SET BPM), a big TAP with NUDGE − / + beside it (hold: the tempo 3 % slower / faster), BPM − / + 1, sections A–D, FILL, BREAK, ALL BACK, mutes. LIVE: the eight drum sounds (lit = in), the DJ filter, REVERB, DELAY, FEEDBACK and BACK TO NORMAL. FX: the FX pad (an effect runs while you touch its key). MIX: the DJ filter, isolator, macros and morph; tap a fader's value and it jumps back to rest. VOICE: type words, the first VOICE track (or the one you pick) sings them from its next note. LFO: draw USER1–4. The remote's CCs are on channel 16 (any MIDI app can send them: 20 PLAY, 22–27 mutes, 28–31 sections, 32 FILL, 33 BREAK, 34 ALL BACK, 35 TAP, 36–37 NUDGE − / + (held), 38–39 BPM − / + 1, 40–66 the FX pad's keys, 70 DJ filter, 71–73 isolator, 74–77 macros, 78 morph, 79 BPM, 80 then 81 an exact BPM in tenths, 85–90 track select, 91–98 the drum sounds out (64+) / in, 99 BACK TO NORMAL, 100 DELAY, 101 REVERB, 102 FEEDBACK).
  53. The volume knob (0.9.9): the FM-1's volume knob is the last thing in the chain, for the headphones and the speaker only. The comp, the limiter, the scopes, spectrum and loudness meters, and the USB audio to Ableton all get the full level whatever the knob is at, so you can keep your ears safe and still drive the limiter and read the meters. USB is always the full level now (lower it in Live, or MENU > USB LEVEL −6 to −24 dB).
  54. Robot voice: pick track 6 (VOICE), press 5 (EDIT) until WORDS. ] throws a new phrase, [ goes back to the last one; the screen shows the word being sung. LANG and LYRIC are on the knobs; THEME and ARTIC are on its first EDIT page. Knob 3 on WORDS is STYLE: HUMAN (as it sings), ROBOT (steady, buzzy, through a ring modulator), GLADOS (the Portal computer: steady, a higher head, every syllable on its own auto-tuned step), TALKBOX (a sawtooth through the mouth, each voice of a chord its own: a synth that talks).
